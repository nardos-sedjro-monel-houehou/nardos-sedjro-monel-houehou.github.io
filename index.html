About Nardos Sedjro Monel Houehou

My name is Nardos Sedjro Monel Houehou (written Houehou Sedjro Nardos Monel in official documents, family name first). Most people call me Nardos.

I'm a full-stack web and application developer. I like the whole chain: understanding how a business really works, designing the data model, building the back end and the interface, then shipping it and making sure the people who use it every day are comfortable with it.

My main tools are Laravel and PHP on the server side, and Next.js, TypeScript and JavaScript on the front end. More and more of my work involves automation and AI: connecting tools together, removing repetitive tasks and adding smart features to existing software.

What I build

  • Custom business software — management tools, client portals and dashboards built around how a team actually works.
  • Websites for small businesses — fast, mobile-first sites with online booking and contact forms.
  • REST APIs and integrations — connecting a website or app to payment, calendar, email and other services.
  • Automation & AI — workflows that save hours every week, and AI features inside existing tools.

How I work

  1. Understand — the business, its customers and its constraints, before writing any code.
  2. Build — clean, documented code, with regular demos so there are no surprises.
  3. Hand over — deployment, short training and documentation so the client stays independent.
